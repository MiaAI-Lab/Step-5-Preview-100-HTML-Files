1

Buy

One lot at a time, enough to last three weeks. We pay 2.4× the commodity price, which is still less than the farm deserves.

2

Roast

Two mornings a week on a 5 kg drum. Every profile is written by hand and abandoned when the beans say otherwise.

3

Rest

Four days minimum for filter, nine for espresso. Then it is at its best for eleven more, and then it is not.

4

Go

Bags leave the shop the same day they are filled, with the roast date on the front and nothing on the back.
