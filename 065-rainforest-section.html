i

Emergent

Two trees in every hectare break the canopy and take full sun. They also take the wind, which is why emergent leaves are small and stiff rather than broad.

ii

Canopy

The roof, and where almost everything lives. It is a nearly continuous surface, so an animal can cross the forest without touching the ground.

iii

Understory

Two percent of the light. Leaves down here are wide and dark to catch what is left, and they drip all night so the floor stays wet.

iv

Forest floor

A half-percent of the light and no wind at all. A leaf that falls takes weeks to rot, because the decomposers are waiting for warmth they never get.

v

The hour

Drag the hour and the whole section is re-lit: dawn is cold and flat, noon is hard and white, dusk is amber through the dust, and night is close to black.

vi

The rain

Rain falls on a canvas laid over the section and is caught by the leaves, so the number that matters is not how hard it falls but how much gets through.
