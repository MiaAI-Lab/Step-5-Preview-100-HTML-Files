# CALIBRE 09 — A Hand-Wound Chronometer

## Short title
Luxury Watch

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a luxury watch product
page, "CALIBRE 09" by the fictional Maison Vaurin. No external dependencies
whatsoever — no CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no external SVG files, no
JavaScript libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works offline by
double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: precious-metal minimalism. Near-black #0a0a0b ground, warm gold
#d6c49f and a darker gold #8f7f5c, off-white #efece5, and hairline gold rules at
18% alpha used to separate everything. No colour other than gold and the dial's
deep blue-black appears anywhere. Type is a high-contrast serif for display and
numbers, a monospace at 8.5–9.5px with 0.2–0.34em tracking for every label, and a
system sans only for one or two paragraphs.

The watch is built in CSS, not drawn. The case is a circle whose background is a
radial highlight over a conic sweep of greys, so it reads as brushed steel catching
light at the top-left. The bezel is a 12-stop conic gradient that alternates
dark and bright gold in an irregular rhythm, giving a fluted, machined look. The
dial is an inset circle of deep blue-black with a fine guilloché — a
repeating-radial-gradient of 1px white lines every 7px — and holds sixty indices
generated in JavaScript (every fifth one bigger and wider), three hands (gold
gradients, the second hand carrying a counterweight tail), a bevelled centre cap,
and two lines of tiny text. Two lugs and two leather-coloured bands with an inner
shadow complete the silhouette. Moving the pointer tilts the whole watch in 3D with
rotateY/rotateX driven by normalised pointer position. The hands are driven by the
real wall clock at animation-frame resolution, including milliseconds, so the
sweep is genuinely smooth.

Below, a second canvas renders the exposed movement. On a dark plate it draws
Côtes de Genève as sixteen concentric engraved rings; twenty-four ruby jewels, each
a radial gradient from pink-white through crimson to near-black, seeded around the
outer ring; and five mechanisms at their true relative speeds — a mainspring barrel
whose spring is an Archimedean spiral of five turns and which rotates slowest, a
centre wheel with six spokes and twenty-eight teeth, a third wheel, a fast
fifteen-spoke escape wheel, and a balance wheel with a four-turn hairspring that
oscillates on a sine rather than rotating, its rim carrying four timing screws. A
bevelled bridge sweeps across the plate with a thick and a thin stroke.

Layout: a sticky nav with the maison name at 0.42em tracking, three links and a
gold "Enquire" button. The hero is a two-column split — reference line, serif
headline with one italic gold word, lede, a six-cell specification grid built from
1px gaps over gold (movement, power reserve, case, crystal, water resistance,
frequency), a large price, and a secondary CTA — against the watch on its stage
inside a soft gold halo. Then the movement section as a mirrored two-column split
with a four-row spec list. Then a four-cell strip of large gold serif numerals
(39.0, 72, 218, 88). Then a ruled monospace footer.

Interaction: the watch tilts toward the pointer, its hands keep real time, the
movement keeps running beneath it, and the two gold buttons confirm in place when
pressed. There are no other controls — the page's whole argument is restraint, and
the only motion is either mechanical (the hands, the wheels) or the tilt.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A precious-metal product page: a CSS-built chronometer — conic-gradient fluted
bezel, guilloché dial, sixty indices and three gold hands keeping genuinely smooth
real time — tilting toward your pointer, above a canvas movement where a spiral
mainspring, three toothed wheels, an escape wheel and an oscillating balance all
turn at their true relative rates, in nothing but gold on near-black.

## Major visual techniques used
- Brushed-steel case from a radial highlight over a conic grey sweep with paired
  inset shadows
- Fluted bezel from an irregular 12-stop conic gradient alternating dark/bright gold
- Guilloché dial from a repeating-radial-gradient of hairlines
- Sixty dial indices generated in JS, every fifth enlarged, rotated into place
- Real-time hands driven at animation-frame resolution including milliseconds
- 3D tilt with rotateY/rotateX from normalised pointer position
- Canvas movement: sixteen concentric Côtes de Genève rings, twenty-four ruby jewels
  as three-stop radial gradients, and a bevelled bridge in two stroke weights
- Archimedean-spiral mainspring (five turns) drawn as a single path
- Toothed wheels from spoke lines plus a ring plus 28 tick marks
- Oscillating balance wheel: four-turn hairspring spiral, rim, two arms and four
  timing screws, rotating on a sine instead of a linear rate
- Spec and stat grids built from 1px gaps over a gold-coloured background
- Extreme typographic restraint: serif display + 8.5px tracked monospace labels
- Sticky nav with backdrop blur; hero and movement collapse to one column under
  880px

## Intended interaction model
An object to be admired. Moving the pointer tilts the watch and reveals its depth;
everything else is already running — real time on the dial, the movement turning
beneath it. The two gold enquiry buttons are the only actions and both simply
confirm. The page has no other interaction because a product at this price is
presented by standing back.
