# LUMEN — Sunrise Console

## Short title
Neumorphic Sunrise Console

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a soft-UI
(neumorphic) control console for a fictional sunrise lamp called "LUMEN". No
external dependencies at all — no CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no
external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works
offline by opening the file, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: soft clay. Every surface is the same warm sand colour as the
page background so nothing reads as a separate object; instead depth comes
entirely from paired shadows — a soft dark shadow down-right and a bright white
shadow up-left — plus inset versions that "press" elements into the material.
Nothing has a border. Nothing is pure white or pure black. The palette is warm
and organic: sand #e7e2d8, terracotta accent #c9784f, sage #8aa39b, warm ink
#3a342b.

Layout: a centred 920px column. Header with a soft extruded orb logo (a
concentric neumorphic sphere with a glowing terracotta pupil) and a pill-shaped
status readout with a breathing dot. Below, a two-column board. Left column: a
large circular dial card. The dial is a conic-gradient arc of terracotta progress
over a pressed-in inner plate, ringed with 24 tick marks, carrying a soft
extruded knob that rides the circumference; its centre shows a large thin
time readout, a phase label, and a small "sun" disc that rises and grows as the
value increases. Under the dial, three soft pill preset buttons (Dawn / Early /
Late). Right column: two stacked cards — a "Light ramp" card with two custom
neumorphic range sliders (duration, peak brightness) and a pressed-in recessed
readout that writes out the resulting schedule in prose, and a "Comfort" card
with a chime toggle, a temperature slider, and a terracotta gradient save button
that raises a floating soft toast.

Typography: one system sans stack throughout, differentiated only by weight and
tracking — 300-weight very large numerals with tabular figures for the time,
600-weight wide-tracked uppercase at 9–11px for every label. Nothing decorative.

Interaction: the dial is genuinely draggable — pointer events map the angle
between the cursor and the dial centre onto a 12-hour span (04:00 → 16:00),
updating the conic arc, knob rotation, time text, phase label, sun height and the
prose readout live; it is also keyboard operable with the arrow keys and exposes
role="slider" with aria values. The presets animate the dial to fixed times. The
sliders reformat their own labels and rewrite the prose readout. The chime pill
presses in and out. Saving raises a toast that fades itself after two seconds.
Everything re-lays out on resize and the two columns stack under 820px.
prefers-reduced-motion is honoured.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A calm, tactile instrument panel carved out of soft clay: one large draggable
neumorphic dial whose terracotta arc, orbiting knob, rising sun and live prose
readout all respond to a single value, surrounded by pressed-in sliders, extruded
pill toggles and a warm sand palette where depth is made only from paired light
and dark shadows.

## Major visual techniques used
- Neumorphism: paired offset shadows (dark down-right, white up-left) with
  background-matched surfaces and zero borders
- Extruded vs. pressed states — inset shadows flip buttons from raised to recessed
- Conic-gradient progress arc driven by a CSS custom property (--p) updated in JS
- 24 radial tick marks placed with rotate() + translateY() around the dial centre
- Draggable radial control using pointer capture, atan2 angle mapping and a
  12-hour normalisation
- Concentric neumorphic sphere logo using layered gradients and inset rings
- Soft glow via small-radius box-shadow rather than blur filters
- Custom-styled range inputs with ::webkit-slider-runnable-track / thumb and
  ::moz equivalents
- Pressed-in "display" panel using inset shadows
- Thin 300-weight tabular-figure typography against 600-weight wide-tracked
  uppercase labels
- Floating soft toast with opacity + translate transition and auto-dismiss
- Two-column board collapsing to one under 820px; prefers-reduced-motion honoured

## Intended interaction model
A single-purpose control surface. The visitor drags (or arrows) the dial to choose
a wake time, nudges the ramp sliders, toggles chimes, and reads the machine
translate those choices into an English sentence describing the light schedule.
Presets offer one-tap shortcuts. Saving is a confirmation gesture only. The whole
thing is meant to feel like turning a physical dial on a warm ceramic object.
