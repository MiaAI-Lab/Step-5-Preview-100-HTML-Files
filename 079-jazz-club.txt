# THE ROOM — a chart, a set, a solo

## Short title
Jazz Club

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a jazz club at night, where four
players sit under one moving spotlight and a solo is generated over a fixed twelve-bar
blues, written onto a staff as it goes. No external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no
web fonts, no external images, no JS libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works
offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: a warm dark room. Ground #0a0806, hairlines rgba(240,230,214,.14, body
#f0e6d6 with muted #a2937c, amber #e8b25c for the light and the chart and sage #8fc7a8 for
in-scale notes, with pink #ff9ecb for the ones that are not. Serif headings with italic
accent words, uppercase .2em–.4em kickers, monospace figures.

The room is one canvas at 1000 × 440: a warm vertical gradient, three stage lights behind
the band, seventy blobs of smoke drifting upward that brighten inside the beam, a cone of
light from a stand to a pool on the floor, and four silhouettes with rim light — three
behind and the soloist, larger, in the beam with a horn whose bell catches the light.

The chart is fixed: twelve bars of a blues in B flat — I, IV, I, I, IV, I, V, IV, I, IV, I,
V — printed in the corner with the current bar marked. The solo is generated from a scale
walk: a step or a leap in the mixolydian set of a seventh chord, with the chromatic slider
raising the chance of an off-scale step. Every note is classed by whether its semitone
position is the root, third, fifth or flat seventh, and the in-scale ones are drawn green on
the staff and the others pink.

The staff is a second canvas with five ruled lines, twelve bar lines, a highlighted bar at
the current position, and each note written at its bar and beat. Take a chorus resets the
chorus, clears the staff and shifts the spotlight.

Controls: tempo, notes per bar, chromatic, Start the set and Take a chorus. Readout: tempo,
key, bar, current chord, the interval of the last note, and choruses taken.

Layout: nav, centred hero, the scene with the chart overlay, the staff, a dock of three
sliders and two buttons, a readout row, then two three-cell note rows on the changes,
tension, chromatic, the spotlight, the staff and a chorus, then a footer.

Interaction: press Start the set and the room begins: the solo walks up and down the scale,
the bar marker moves across the staff, the chart marks the chord underneath, and the
spotlight drifts with the phrasing. Visitors set the tempo, thin or thicken the notes, push
the chromatic up to make the line lean hard against the changes, and take another chorus for
a fresh start.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A jazz club on canvas where four silhouettes sit under a cone of light through drifting
smoke, and a solo is generated over a fixed twelve-bar blues in B flat — a scale walk over
the mixolydian set of a seventh chord whose off-scale notes are marked pink and in-scale
ones green — written onto a ruled staff as the bar marker crosses it, with tempo, density
and chromatic sliders and a chorus button that clears the staff and shifts the spotlight.

## Major visual techniques used
- Room as a warm vertical gradient with three stage lights behind, so the space reads as
  lit rather than dark
- Smoke as seventy drifting radial blobs whose alpha rises inside the beam, which is what
  makes a small drawing read as air
- Beam as a cone from a stand to a pool on the floor, with a radial pool gradient at the
  base
- Figures as filled silhouettes with a single rim-light stroke on the beam side, so depth
  comes from one edge rather than shading
- The soloist drawn larger with a horn whose bell is the brightest thing in the frame
- Chart printed as HTML in the corner with the current bar marked, from the same array
  the clock uses
- Solo generated as a walk over the mixolydian set of a seventh chord, with leap and
  chromatic probabilities from the sliders
- Notes classed by semitone position, in-scale drawn green and off-scale pink, so tension
  is visible
- Staff drawn from the same note array as the scene, with bar lines and a highlighted bar
- Sub-beat clock with a catch-up guard, so a slow tempo and a fast one both work
- Beam drift tracked by easing the spotlight toward a target that moves per chorus
- Interval readout derived from the last note's semitone position, so the readout is a
  property of the line

## Intended interaction model
A room you run. Visitors press Start the set, then set the tempo, thin or thicken the notes,
and push the chromatic slider to make the line lean against the chord underneath — watching
the staff fill green where it is safe and pink where it is not — and take another chorus to
clear the staff and move the light.
