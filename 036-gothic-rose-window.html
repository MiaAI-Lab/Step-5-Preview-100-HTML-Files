Sainte-Croix · north transept
  • The rose

c. 1230 · rayonnant

Twelve petals,
and the hour.

Made to hold the four o'clock light for about eleven minutes a day. The tracery turns; the glass does not, because the glass is the wall.

Diameter 8.4 m · twelve principal tracery bars
132 panels · no two the same colour

8.4

metres across

Set into 60 cm of masonry, the tracery bars are sandstone, each a single piece worked on both faces.

132

panels

Painted and fired on site, then leaded into the bars. The reds are copper; the blues are cobalt.

4

o'clock, daily

For a few minutes the inner ring projects itself across the floor of the transept, and then it is gone.
