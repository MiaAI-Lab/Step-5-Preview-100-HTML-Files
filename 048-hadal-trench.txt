# TRENCH — The Hadal Zone, 6 000 m

## Short title
Hadal Trench

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a hadal-trench
biome scene called TRENCH. No external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no web
fonts, no external images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS
and JavaScript inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: the deepest water on the planet. An abyss gradient (#01131c →
#010c14 → #01080e) with a teal wash at the floor, whose CSS brightness is
recomputed every frame from the Life slider so the whole page breathes between
populated and empty. The light source is a hydrothermal vent at the bottom centre:
a warm orange radial whose radius scales with the Plume slider, over which forty
dark smoker particles rise on a hash-driven position, wrapping vertically.

Three animal populations, each drawn in additive blend. Marine snow falls upward
when the Current slider is positive, drifting on per-particle sines. Fine motes are
pushed horizontally by the current and gently attracted toward the pointer within
220 pixels, so a cursor gathers them the way a submersible's beam gathers
plankton — the page's one physical joke. Up to sixteen jellyfish drift past on
their own velocities; each has a bell whose radius pulses on its own phase, five
internal ribs, and six to thirteen tentacles drawn as four-segment sine-wobbling
curves behind it, all tinted either hue 195 (cyan) or hue 275 (violet). Each jelly
has a random burst state that decays at 0.93 per frame, thickening its bell when
firing.

Layout: a full-viewport canvas over the abyss with a floating UI overlay whose
pointer-events pass through except on the controls. Top-left is a monospace label
above an italic serif headline with one line in bioluminescent pink, plus a short
paragraph. Top-right is a monospace gauge stack showing depth, pressure and
temperature, each derived live from the current setting. Bottom is a glass tray
with three sliders (Current 0–100, Life 0–100, Plume 0–100) plus Vent and Calm
buttons, and a monospace hint.

Interaction: the Current slider pushes everything sideways and pulls marine snow
upward; Life changes the population counts (rebuilding all three arrays) and the
page brightness; Plume scales the vent glow and the smoker density. Vent toggles
the whole thermal feature off. Calm zeroes everything and rebuilds, which is the
calmest the trench ever gets. Moving the pointer gathers the motes. On narrow
screens the paragraph, gauges and hint hide.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A hydrothermal trench: a vent glowing at the floor with dark smoker particles
rising through it, marine snow carried upward by the current, fine motes that
gather toward your cursor, and up to sixteen pulsing jellyfish with sine-wobbling
tentacles in cyan and violet — all additively blended over an abyss gradient whose
brightness, population and vent scale are retunable live.

## Major visual techniques used
- Warm vent radial whose radius and alpha scale with the Plume slider
- Forty smoker particles positioned from an integer hash, wrapping vertically
- Marine snow pushed upward by the current, on per-particle sine drift
- Motes gathered toward the pointer within a 220-pixel radius, plus horizontal
  current push
- Jellyfish bells whose radius pulses on a per-animal phase, with five internal
  ribs drawn as quadratic curves
- Tentacles as four-segment sine-wobbling curves drawn behind the bell
- Two-colour jelly palette (hue 195 cyan, hue 275 violet) split across the population
- Burst states that thicken the bell and decay at 0.93 per frame
- Additive compositing ('lighter') for all three populations
- CSS abyss gradient whose brightness is recomputed per frame from Life
- Warm floor rim gradient at the bottom 18% of the frame
- Live gauges: depth (6012 + current), pressure and temperature, each derived from
  the current setting
- Calm preset that zeroes all three sliders, turns the vent off and rebuilds the
  populations
- Italic serif headline with one pink line against 8.5px tracked monospace
- Floating glass tray with backdrop-filter blur; extras hidden under 760px

## Intended interaction model
A scene you can tune. Visitors set the current, the density of life and the vent
activity, then watch the trench repopulate itself; moving the pointer gathers the
motes. Vent strips the heat away, and Calm empties everything to the stillest
possible frame. The populations rebuild whenever Life changes, so the page responds
to every slider immediately.
