# THE GEODE — A Chamber of Slow Light

## Short title
Isometric Crystal Cave

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: an interactive crystal
cave called THE GEODE. No external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no web fonts,
no external images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and
JavaScript inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: a dark geode chamber lit from within. The ground is a radial
gradient from violet #1a1030 through #0d0819 to near-black, with a CSS radial
vignette on top. Inside it, sixteen to twenty-six crystal shards stand at random
positions, each drawn as a tall hexagonal prism — a six-point path with a pointed
base and a chamfered shoulder — filled with a three-stop HSL gradient that is
bright on the upper-left and nearly transparent on the lower-right, stroked with a
thin bright edge in its own hue, crossed by internal cleavage lines (two diagonals
and a vertical spine), and crowned with a soft specular radial pip near its tip.
Each shard carries its own hue from a five-colour set (violet 268, teal 172, rose
344, gold 42, blue 200), its own saturation and lightness, and its own slow
sinoidal drift.

The depth system: shards are assigned a layer from 0 to 1 and sorted so the far
ones draw first. Each shard's parallax response to the pointer is multiplied by its
layer value plus a base lag, so near crystals swing a long way and distant ones
barely move — the chamber has real depth. A depth slider overlays a near-black wash
whose alpha rises to 0.55, so pushing depth up genuinely buries the chamber in
darkness. A light slider scales every gradient's alpha, edge brightness and
specular pip together, and above 40% adds a violet bloom over the whole viewport.
Ninety dust motes float on their own per-mote phase and layer-scaled parallax.

Layout: a full-viewport canvas with a floating UI overlay (pointer-events pass
through except on controls). Top-left is a monospace chamber label above an italic
serif headline whose second line carries a teal→violet→rose gradient clipped to the
text, plus a short paragraph. Top-right is a monospace legend showing the live
facet count, depth value and light percentage. Bottom is a glass tray with three
sliders — Depth, Light and Drift — and a "New chamber" button that regenerates the
whole arrangement, beside a monospace hint.

Interaction: moving the pointer looks around the chamber (eased, so the crystals
answer with a slight delay); dragging horizontally rotates every shard by a small
layer-scaled amount so the whole chamber turns; the wheel pushes the camera in and
out between 0.5× and 2.4×, scaling shard size and parallax together. The three
sliders retune the running scene live, and "New chamber" rebuilds it. On narrow
screens the paragraph, legend and hint hide so the crystals fill the device.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A dark geode chamber of hexagonal crystal shards, each with its own hue, drift and
parallax lag, so the room has real depth: moving the pointer looks around it,
dragging turns it, the wheel pushes into it, and depth, light and drift sliders
bury, brighten and stir the whole cave live.

## Major visual techniques used
- Hexagonal crystal prisms drawn as six-point paths with pointed base and chamfered
  shoulder
- Three-stop HSL body gradients with alpha driven by the light slider
- Thin bright edge strokes in each shard's own hue, dimmed by layer
- Internal cleavage lines: two diagonals plus a vertical spine
- Specular pip from a radial gradient near each shard's tip
- Layer-sorted drawing order with per-layer parallax lag for real depth
- Eased pointer look-around, drag rotation and wheel zoom (0.5×–2.4×)
- Depth as a full-viewport near-black wash at up to 0.55 alpha
- Violet bloom overlay above 40% light
- 90 dust motes on independent phases with layer-scaled parallax
- Procedural chamber regeneration with randomised count, position, size, hue,
  saturation, lightness, rotation and drift
- Italic serif headline with a gradient-clipped second line against 8.5px tracked
  monospace labels
- Floating glass tray with backdrop-filter blur; paragraph, legend and hint hidden
  under 760px

## Intended interaction model
A place to look around. Visitors move the pointer to shift their viewpoint through
the chamber, drag to turn it, scroll to push deeper into it, and use the three
sliders to change how dark, how bright and how restless the cave is. "New chamber"
throws the whole arrangement away and grows another. Nothing on the page has a
correct state.
