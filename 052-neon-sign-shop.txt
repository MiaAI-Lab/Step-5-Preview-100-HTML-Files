# NEON & GLASS — A Sign Shop

## Short title
Neon Sign Shop

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a fictional neon sign
shop called NEON & GLASS, whose centrepiece is a wall of working neon signs. No
external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no
external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works
offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: dark walls and hot glass. The page ground is near-black violet
#0b0710 with panels at #171028; the sign wall is a radial violet wash over a dark
linear gradient with a hundred-pixel inset shadow so it reads as a recessed shop
window, and a brick course faintly visible behind at 14% opacity (two intersecting
repeating-linear-gradients). Five colours do all the luminous work: rose #ff4fa3,
ice cyan #3ee0ff, lime #b8ff5c, amber #ffb45e and smoke #c8a8ff.

The neon effect is built from three stacked layers on one word. The word itself is
coloured with the sign's hue and given four stacked text-shadows at 4, 12, 28 and
60 pixels in the same colour — which is exactly how neon reads: a tight core with
progressively wider halos. A `.tube` layer sits absolutely over the same word with
a 1.5px near-white text-stroke and white and coloured glows at 55% opacity, so the
letterforms read as actual glass tubing with a bright wall and a coloured bleed. And
a `.sign::after` glow — a radial of the sign's own colour at 10% with a 14px blur,
inset behind the word — provides the overall bloom that lights the brick around it.
Each word runs a five-second flicker animation keyed at 41–43% and 77–81%, so the
sign stutters like a real transformer failing. Turning a sign off collapses all
three layers to almost nothing.

Five signs hang on the wall at descending sizes and five different colours: Motel
(huge, rose), Vacancy (large, cyan, italic), Open Late (lime, wide-tracked,
ultralight sans), 24 Hours (amber) and a small italic pink "diner · next left".

Layout: a nav with a serif brand and three links. A centred hero with a cyan
eyebrow, a serif headline with one italic pink word, and a paragraph about bending
glass. Then the sign wall with its brick, the five signs, a hint line and three
tool buttons. Then a five-cell colour swatch row (one per gas fill, each a circle
glowing in its own colour). Then a three-cell notes row with large pink serif
figures (850 degrees, 12 000 volts, 1 movement per letter). Then a ruled monospace
footer.

Interaction: clicking any sign switches it off and on again. "All on" and "All off"
set every sign at once. "Random flicker" starts an interval that toggles signs at
random every 340 milliseconds until pressed again (its label becomes "Stop
flicker"). The notes and swatches collapse to one column under 820px.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A neon shop wall in a recessed dark window: five glass words — huge rose Motel,
cyan italic Vacancy, wide-tracked lime Open Late, amber 24 Hours and a small pink
arrow — each built from a four-layer stack of stacked text-shadows, a white
text-stroke tube overlay and a blurred colour bloom, all stuttering on a five-second
flicker, switchable individually or all at once, with a random-flicker mode.

## Major visual techniques used
- Four-layer neon stack: coloured word, four stacked text-shadows (4/12/28/60px), a
  white 1.5px text-stroke tube overlay at 55%, and a blurred 10% colour bloom
- Five-second flicker animation keyed at two points (41–43% and 77–81%) to imitate a
  failing transformer
- Off state collapsing all three glow layers to near-zero
- Brick course from two intersecting repeating-linear-gradients at 14% opacity
- Recessed window from a radial violet wash, a dark linear gradient and a
  hundred-pixel inset shadow
- Sign sizing hierarchy from 84px down to 26px across five colours
- Mixed typefaces across the signs: serif, italic serif, ultralight wide-tracked sans
- Swatch row where each circle glows in its own colour via a box-shadow in
  currentColor
- All on / All off / Random flicker controls, with the flicker button rewording
  itself and stopping the interval
- Notes grid of large pink serif figures built from 1px gaps
- Dark palette with one dominant pink accent and four supporting hues
- Responsive: notes and swatches to one column under 820px

## Intended interaction model
A wall you can operate. Visitors click individual signs to switch them off and on,
or use the three buttons to light the whole wall, darken it, or put it into a random
flicker that stutters like a bad transformer. Everything else on the page is the
shop's own copy — the wall is the interface.
