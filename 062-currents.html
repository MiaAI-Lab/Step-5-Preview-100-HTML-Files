Nothing moves but the lines

A field you can stir
with a fingertip

Every particle here is advected: it reads the direction of the field where it stands, takes one step, and paints a hairline as it goes. The picture is the accumulated history of thousands of those decisions.

i

Advection

Read the direction at the particle, move a fraction of it, repeat. Nothing is orbiting on a circle and nothing is sine-waving; the lines come out of the field, not out of a preset.

ii

The field

Two angles summed at different scales, which is the cheapest honest way to get turbulence. Turn the scale down and the vortices tighten; turn it up and the sheet turns into a few broad rivers.

iii

The drag

Particles are pulled a fraction of the way toward the cursor each frame, so dragging through the field leaves a wake long after the pointer has gone.

iv

Palette

Line colour is chosen by speed, not by particle, so a fast filament changes hue as it accelerates and the same particles draw a thermal map of themselves.

v

Arrows

Show the field and the sheet acquires a lattice of short arrows sampled from the same function — the same numbers, drawn rather than traced.

vi

Decay

The whole surface is washed once per frame with a translucent ground, so old streams fade at the same rate everywhere instead of cheating per particle.
