About

MATERIAL Ninety-nine sheets of 400gsm uncoated board, printed letterpress in a single ink on a 1948 Heidelberg. The impression is visible. That is the point.

METHOD Every poster is generated from one rule: divide the sheet, fill one field, leave the rest alone. Ninety-nine artists were given the same rule and no other instruction.

VISIT Tuesday to Sunday, 11:00 — 18:00. Thursday until 21:00. The stairwell installation is best viewed from the landing, looking up.
