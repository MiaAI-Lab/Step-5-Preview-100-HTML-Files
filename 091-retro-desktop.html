i

Eight apps

Word, Draw, Graph, Terminal, Calc, Notes, Control and Trash. Each is a real window with a real title bar, a real zoom box and a real close box, and each draws its own contents rather than showing a picture of them.

ii

The system font

Chicago and Geneva were drawn at specific pixel sizes for specific displays. Cycling the font size slider is the same effect as changing the screen resolution, which is what people actually did.

iii

Colour depth

Eight colours, sixteen, two hundred and fifty-six or thousands — the palette the whole desktop is drawn from. The buttons row switches the whole machine's colour depth, and every window re-draws.

iv

Dragging

Windows drag by their title bar and nothing else, they stack in the order you opened them, and closing one brings the one below forward. There is no compositor here — just a list and a redraw.

v

Phosphor

Nothing burns in permanently on an LCD, so the persistence is faked: the last frame dims into the new one, which is what a long-persistence tube does.

vi

Reboot

Reboot closes every window, wipes the desktop and starts again with the boot icon, which is the only honest way to describe what a restart did.
