i

The chamber

A melt with gas dissolved in it under pressure. Push it down and the gas stays in solution; take the lid off and it comes out of solution like a shaken bottle, and the melt becomes a foam that can be thrown a kilometre.

ii

Viscosity

Runny basalt lets gas leave politely and flows away as lava. Sticky rhyolite holds the gas until it fails, and fails explosively. The slider is the whole difference between Hawaii and Tambora.

iii

Cooling

Lava that stops moving starts growing a crust, and the crust thickens until the flow is a plug. The flows in this section keep their shape once they set, so you can watch the edifice grow outward.

iv

Ash

An eruption that fragments the melt makes a column, and the column falls back outward, so the deposit thins with distance. Here the fallout simply drifts downwind and fades.

v

Seismic

Rock cracking makes the swarm that comes first. This section has no sound, but the tremor line under the mountain moves with the pressure, which is the same signal.

vi

Quiet

Go quiet and the system relaxes: gas bleeds off, the magma drains back down, and the vent seals. A volcano spends almost all of its time in this state.
