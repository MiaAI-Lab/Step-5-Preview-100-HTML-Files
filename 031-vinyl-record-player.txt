# LACQUER — Side A, Take One

## Short title
Vinyl Record Player

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a vinyl record product
page with a working turntable called LACQUER. No external dependencies whatsoever
— no CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript
libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive,
valid HTML5. No audio is used: the record is visual and kinetic only.

Artistic direction: a dark listening room. Ground #14100f with a warm radial light
from the upper left, paper-cream text #efe9dd, gold #c9a227 as the single accent,
and proof red #b8342a on one catalog number and one italic word. Type is a
high-contrast serif for headings and the paper label, a system sans for paragraphs,
and 8.5–9.5px wide-tracked monospace for all metadata.

The turntable is built in CSS. The platter is a circle with a radial highlight and
paired inset shadows (a warm one above, a deep one below) plus a large soft drop
shadow. The disc sits inside it in near-black, with its grooves drawn as a
repeating-radial-gradient of 1px white lines every 4px at 3.5% alpha, and a light
sweep drawn as a conic-gradient of two faint warm arcs so the dead wax appears to
catch the room light as it turns. The paper label at the centre is a radial cream
gradient with the artist name in serif, an italic red catalog line, and a 7px
monospace speed line. A chrome spindle sits on top. The tone arm is a shaft with a
rounded pivot at one end and a black cartridge head at the other, with a gold stylus
pip, hinged at 92% of its width; at rest it parks at −24° and swings to +6° when the
needle drops, over 1.4 seconds on a slow easing curve.

The disc spins with a CSS animation whose duration is set from the selected speed —
1.8 seconds at the default (33⅓ rpm), recomputed for 45 and 78 rpm and for "stopped"
— and the animation is paused and resumed via animation-play-state rather than being
restarted, so changing speed mid-spin does not jerk the record.

Layout: a nav with a monospace brand and two links. A two-column deck: the platter
on the left, and on the right a gold eyebrow, a serif headline with one italic gold
word, a paragraph, a six-track list where each row shows a number, title and duration
and turns gold when selected, and a transport row with Drop the needle / Pause, Lift
the arm, and a speed slider showing stopped / 33⅓ / 45 / 78. Then a four-cell spec
strip built from 1px gaps with large gold serif figures. Then a ruled monospace
footer.

Interaction: clicking any track selects it, swings the arm in and starts the platter
turning. Drop the needle starts the record (loading the first track if none is
chosen) and becomes Pause; Lift the arm stops it and parks the arm. The speed slider
changes the rotation rate live, including a "stopped" position. The space bar
toggles play and pause. The deck and specs collapse to one column and two columns
respectively under 880px.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A listening-room record page whose turntable is real CSS: a platter with paired
inset shadows, a near-black disc with a repeating-radial groove texture and a
conic light sweep, a cream paper label, a hinged tone arm with a gold stylus that
swings in on a 1.4-second easing when you pick a track, and a platter whose rotation
rate you can change between stopped, 33⅓, 45 and 78 without restarting the spin.

## Major visual techniques used
- Platter depth from a radial highlight plus paired inset shadows and a wide soft
  drop shadow
- Groove texture from a repeating-radial-gradient of 1px lines every 4px at 3.5% alpha
- Light sweep across the wax from a conic-gradient of two faint warm arcs
- Cream paper label as a radial gradient with serif type, an italic red line and a
  7px monospace speed line
- Chrome spindle as a small radial-gradient circle on a higher z-index
- Tone arm hinged at 92% width, with a shaft gradient, a rounded pivot and a black
  cartridge head with a 2px gold stylus indicator
- Arm swing from −24° to +6° over 1.4s on a slow cubic-bezier
- Rotation rate changed by rewriting animation-duration, with play/pause done via
  animation-play-state so the spin never restarts
- Track list with gold selection state and monospace durations
- Speed slider mapping to stopped / 33⅓ / 45 / 78 rpm
- Space-bar transport toggle
- Dark warm palette with a single gold accent and one proof-red word
- Serif display with 0.3em monospace branding; 1px-gap spec strip
- Two-column deck collapsing under 880px

## Intended interaction model
A turntable you operate. Visitors pick a track (or press Drop the needle) to swing
the arm in and start the platter, Lift the arm to park it, and use the speed slider
to change how fast the record turns — right down to stopped. Space does the same as
the play button. The whole interaction is choosing a track and setting a speed;
everything else on the page is the sleeve.
