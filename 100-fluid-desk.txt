# The Fluid Desk

## Short title
The Fluid Desk

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A warm paper-coloured desk with one black tray at its centre and six fluids to put in it,
each running the equation that describes it rather than an animation that resembles it.
Ink diffusion and the smoke plume are both the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations
solved on a 128-square grid by the stable-fluids recipe — advect, diffuse, project out the
divergence with a Jacobi pressure solve — with buoyancy added for the smoke and a hanging
plate in the flow so a vortex street sheds behind it on its own. The watercolour wash
carries three pigment concentrations with different diffusion rates and granulation on a
paper that dries at its edges, so edge darkening appears rather than being drawn.
Ferrofluid is a container of dipoles pulled toward a magnet you move, spiking along the
field above the Rosensweig threshold. The lava lamp is buoyant ink that rises, cools and
falls. The soap films obey Young–Laplace: the pressure difference across a film is 4σ/r,
so the small bubble empties into the large one until one film remains. Fields can be read
as dye, vorticity, speed, pressure or divergence, and the type is a quiet old-style serif
on paper with a teal and rust accent.

## Major visual techniques used
- Stable fluids: semi-Lagrangian advection, Jacobi diffusion, pressure projection
- Staggered-free grid with one cell of padding and free-slip / no-slip boundary handling
- A solid mask applied as cells where velocity and dye are zeroed, giving real wall drag
- Interleaved three-channel dye array so one buffer carries the watercolour pigments
- PutImageData of a 129-square grid scaled by the canvas, DPR-aware, no smoothing
- Non-fluid trays: dipole force with cohesion, buoyancy with cooling, and film pressure
- Vorticity, speed, pressure and divergence read directly out of the solver, not faked

## Intended interaction model
Six tray chips switch fluid and reseed the grid; five field chips choose what the tray
shows. Three sliders drive viscosity, pointer force and time step live; Pause freezes time
and Clear reseeds. Dragging in the tray splats force and dye in the direction of motion;
for ferrofluid the pointer is the magnet itself. Keys 1–6 choose a tray, f cycles the
field, space pauses and c clears. A HUD readout reports the current field value and the
step count.
