# ROSE — North Transept, Light at Four O'Clock

## Short title
Gothic Rose Window

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a page about a Gothic
rose window, called ROSE, whose tracery turns. No external dependencies whatsoever
— no CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript
libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive,
valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: warm limestone and painted glass. The page ground is stone
#e8e2d4, the window surround is a radial gradient of the same stone into #cfc6b2
and #b9af99, and the glass area is a warm daylight gradient (#fdf6e4 → #f0e0bc →
#e2cba0) so the panels read as light coming through. Ink is #2a2620, the tracery is
drawn in translucent lead rgba(42,38,32,.72), and the single accent is a deep
oxblood red #8c2f2f used for one italic word in the headline, the eyebrow and the
figures. Type is a high-contrast serif for display and figures, with a 9–9.5px
wide-tracked monospace for all labels and captions.

The window is built from DOM elements, not canvas. Twelve principal petals are
placed around the centre with rotate(a)deg translate(R·0.30), each a rotated square
clipped to a diamond with clip-path:polygon(50% 0, 100% 50%, 50% 100%, 0 50%),
filled with a radial gradient that runs from the lighter to the darker of its
colour pair, and composited in screen blend against the daylight ground so it reads
as light passing through. A smaller inner petal in the plain lighter colour sits
inside each, and a ring of twenty-four small diamond panels runs around the hub,
each in one of six medieval glass colours (copper red, cobalt blue, green, ochre,
purple, orange), their light and dark tones taken from the same pair.

The tracery is a separate layer: twelve thick principal bars radiating from the hub
at 3px, twelve thinner secondary bars at 1.5px offset halfway between them, and
thirty-six fine spokes at 1px reaching into the inner ring — all short rectangles
rotated into place with transform-origin at the centre. Two concentric rings of
lead (2px and 1px) and a stone hub with a lead ring complete the structure.

The animation: the glass layer rotates slowly in one direction while the two lead
rings counter-rotate at different rates, all driven from one accumulating time
value in radians — so the tracery and the glass move against each other at
different speeds, which is exactly how a rose window reads as you walk past it.

Layout: a nav with a monospace brand and two links. A centred hero with an
oxblood eyebrow, a serif headline with one italic oxblood word, and a paragraph
about four o'clock light. Then a square stage holding the window, followed by a
monospace caption (diameter, twelve bars, 132 panels). Then a three-cell columns
row built from 1px gaps with large oxblood serif figures. Then a ruled monospace
footer.

Interaction: essentially none — the window counter-rotates continuously and there
are no controls. The page's one real behaviour is that it rebuilds the entire
window on resize, since every petal and bar is sized from the measured glass
radius. The columns collapse to one under 820px.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A stained-glass rose window built from DOM: twelve diamond-clipped petals in six
medieval colour pairs screen-blended over a warm daylight ground, an inner ring of
twenty-four small panels, and three tiers of lead tracery — twelve thick bars,
twelve thin ones, thirty-six fine spokes — with the glass rotating one way and the
two lead rings counter-rotating against it.

## Major visual techniques used
- Diamond petals from clip-path:polygon(50% 0, 100% 50%, 50% 100%, 0 50%) on
  rotated squares
- Rotational placement with rotate(a)deg + translate(R·k) and transform-origin at
  the centre
- Screen blend compositing over a warm daylight gradient so panels read as light

- Radial gradient fills running from lighter to darker tone within each petal
- Three tiers of lead tracery: 3px principal bars, 1.5px secondary bars offset
  halfway, 1px spokes into the inner ring
- Two concentric lead rings at 2px and 1px, plus a stone hub with a lead ring
- Counter-rotation: glass one way, the two rings the other, from one accumulating
  radian time value
- Stone surround from a radial gradient with a soft drop shadow and inset
  highlight
- Full rebuild on resize from the measured glass radius
- Six-colour palette with a light/dark pair per colour
- Serif display with one oxblood italic word against 9px tracked monospace
- Stone-and-lead palette with a single oxblood accent
- Three-column facts row built from 1px gaps, collapsing under 820px

## Intended interaction model
A generated artefact to look at. The window turns continuously; there are no
controls and no input. Resizing the window rebuilds every petal and bar from the
new radius, which is the page's only responsive behaviour beyond the layout
collapse.
