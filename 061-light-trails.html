i

The fade

Nothing is ever cleared. A wash of near-black is laid over the whole surface each frame, so old strokes sink instead of vanishing, and the decay slider is how long the shutter stays open.

ii

The fold

Each point is drawn several times, rotated around the centre and mirrored back, which is how a wobble in your hand becomes a rosette. Two is a butterfly, eight is a kaleidoscope.

iii

The drift

Every stroke remembers where it started and leans back toward it, so a line you leave alone keeps moving after you have stopped drawing.

iv

Light

Strokes are added with a lighter composite rather than painted, so overlapping colours add up the way light does instead of covering each other.

v

Matte

One tool works the other way round and takes light away, which is the only way to carve detail back out of an over-exposed frame.

vi

Sparks

The spark tool emits short-lived particles with gravity and drag as you draw, so the trail gains a comet's tail without any of it being scheduled.
