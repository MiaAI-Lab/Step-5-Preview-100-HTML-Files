# ORIGAMI — Eight Folds to a Crane

## Short title
Origami Folding Studio

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: an origami instruction
studio called ORIGAMI. No external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no web fonts,
no external images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and
JavaScript inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: a bright workshop table. Warm paper #f5f2e8 with a slightly
deeper paper #e6e0cf, true ink #1e1c18, hairline rules at 16% ink, and one proof
red #b8342a used for the step numbers, one italic word in the headline and the
large figures in the facts row. Type is a high-contrast serif for headings and
instructions, a system sans for paragraphs, and a 9–9.5px wide-tracked monospace
for all labels and metadata.

The model: a square "sheet" in a 1400px perspective stage, built from two faces —
a front and a back rotated 180° — each carrying a crease pattern drawn purely with
four overlapping linear-gradients (a vertical crease at 50%, a horizontal one, and
two diagonals) in ink at 12–22% alpha. The front face holds four triangular flaps,
one per quadrant, each with its own transform-origin at the appropriate corner so
it hinges on the right edge. As the sequence advances, the whole sheet tilts
(rotateX from 0° down to 10°, rotateY from 0° to −64°) while the front face spins
(rotateY 0° to 180°), and the flaps fold in one by one — each becoming visible at
the step where it is first folded and rotating to flat. Every transition runs for
1.1 seconds on a slow cubic-bezier(.5, 0, .3, 1) so the motion reads as paper
hinging rather than an element animating.

The sequence: nine steps from square to crane — square, fold in half, fold again,
preliminary base, petals in, narrow and repeat, neck and tail, head, the crane —
each with an instruction sentence and its own sheet rotation, face rotation and
flap list. The current step is highlighted by an italic shift and a padding-left
nudge; completed steps have their numbers turn from red to ink.

Layout: a nav with a serif brand at 0.28em tracking and two links. A centred hero
with a red eyebrow, a serif headline with one italic red word, and a paragraph. Then
a studio section, a 1.2fr/1fr split between the perspective stage and the numbered
step list. Then a tray of three buttons — Previous fold, Next fold and Fold for me.
Then a three-cell facts row built from 1px gaps, with large red serif figures. Then
a ruled monospace footer.

Interaction: the step list is clickable — choose any step and the sheet turns to
match it, folding every flap up to and including that step. Previous and Next walk
the sequence one step at a time. "Fold for me" auto-advances every 2.4 seconds until
it reaches the crane, then stops and returns its label to "Fold for me" (pressing
it again pauses). All animation honours prefers-reduced-motion by collapsing the
transition duration. The studio and facts collapse to one column under 880px.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
An instruction studio where the paper really moves: a CSS-3D square with a
gradient-drawn crease pattern and four hinged triangular flaps, tilting and turning
through nine steps from square to crane, each step folding another flap flat over
1.1 seconds of slow paper-hinging motion, driven by a clickable step list, prev/next
buttons or a self-running "fold for me".

## Major visual techniques used
- CSS 3D: a perspective stage, a preserve-3d sheet, a back face rotated 180°, and a
  front face rotating through 180 degrees
- Crease pattern drawn from four overlapping linear-gradients (vertical, horizontal
  and two diagonal hairlines) at 12–22% ink alpha — no images
- Four triangular flaps with per-corner transform-origins so each hinges correctly
- Staged fold-in: flaps become visible and rotate flat at their step
- Slow 1.1s cubic-bezier(.5,0,.3,1) transitions so motion reads as paper
- Sheet rotation choreographed across the sequence (rotateX 0°→10°, rotateY 0°→−64°)
- Soft elliptical contact shadow under the sheet via a radial-gradient ::after
- Clickable step list with current-step italic emphasis and completed-step number
  recolouring
- Auto-advance interval that stops at the end and restores its label
- Warm paper palette with hairline rules and a single proof-red accent
- Serif display with 0.28em tracking against 9px tracked monospace labels
- 1px-gap facts grid; 1.2fr/1fr studio split collapsing under 880px
- prefers-reduced-motion collapsing all fold transitions

## Intended interaction model
An instruction manual you can operate. Visitors step forward and back through the
folds, jump straight to any step by clicking it, or let the page fold for itself at
a walking pace. The paper responds to every step with a single slow turn, and the
list keeps track of where you are; there is nothing else to do but fold.
