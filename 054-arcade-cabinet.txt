# ARCADE — Cabinet 07, 1983

## Short title
Arcade Cabinet 07

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a 1983 arcade cabinet whose
screen is a real, playable game of Snake. No external dependencies whatsoever — no
CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no JS libraries. All CSS and JavaScript
inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: a dark arcade floor at #0a0c14 with one green light source. The
cabinet is a vertical metal gradient (#1b2133 to #0c0f18) with a 40px green glow
spread beneath it as if the marquee is lighting the machine. Palette: phosphor green
#4dff9b, amber #ffc44d, alert red #ff4d6a, cold cyan #4dd2ff.

The marquee is set in italic serif green with three stacked text-shadows at 6, 20 and
46 pixels, so the title reads as a backlit plastic header; beneath it a mono
sub-line and a faint vertical light band from a ::after gradient. The screen is a
300×300 canvas with `image-rendering:pixelated`, stroked twice — once with a
translucent border and once with a 6px black inset so it reads as a recessed bezel —
and covered by a CRT treatment: 3px repeating scanlines and a radial vignette,
composited with mix-blend-mode:multiply.

The game itself is a 20×20 grid at 15px cells, drawn every frame with a faint green
grid, a food pellet that pulses with Date.now()/160, and a snake whose head is solid
green with a 10px blur glow while the trailing segments fade linearly from 85% to 30%
green alpha. Every cell is a rounded rect drawn through a hand-rolled quadratic
curve helper. Score and high score are zero-padded to four digits like a real cabinet.

Control panel: a recessed round joystick well (inset dark shadow top, faint light
bounce bottom) with a red ball top that is a pseudo-element, and two amber round
buttons with a hard 4px bottom shadow and an inset highlight, which travel 3px and
collect a dark inset shadow on :active. Then a pill "Insert coin" button that inverts
amber on hover. Below the cabinet: Start, Pause, Reset buttons.

Layout: nav with serif brand. Centred hero with green eyebrow and an italic green
word. Then the cabinet in full. Then a three-cell floor-notes row with large green
serif figures (25¢, 19 cabinets, 412 points). Then a ruled mono footer.

Interaction: arrow keys or WASD turn the snake (reversing into itself is refused);
Space toggles pause or starts; R restarts. The on-screen joystick is draggable with
pointer capture — drag or click a direction to turn. Button A pauses/starts, Button B
starts. "Insert coin" and "Start game" both start; "Pause" toggles; "Reset" clears
and returns to Ready. On an attract screen (before the first start) a 90ms interval
repaints the board so the food keeps pulsing. Dying shows "Game over" and freezes the
snake where it lies. The high score persists for the session and updates live.
The floor-notes grid collapses to one column under 820px.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A 1983 cabinet standing on a dark arcade floor: a green backlit marquee over a
scanned, vignetted 20×20 pixel canvas where a real game of Snake runs, the snake's
head glowing green and its segments fading down the tail, four-digit zero-padded
score and high score, a recessed joystick well with a red ball top and two amber
buttons, all driveable by arrows, WASD, dragging the stick, or the on-screen
controls.

## Major visual techniques used
- Phosphor-green marquee from three stacked text-shadows at 6, 20 and 46 pixels
- CRT screen: `image-rendering:pixelated` canvas, 3px repeating scanlines and a
  radial vignette with mix-blend-mode:multiply
- Bezelled screen from a translucent border plus a 6px inset black box-shadow
- Snake drawn with a fading alpha gradient along its length and a 10px blurred glow
  on the head only
- Food pellet pulsing from Date.now()/160 in the sine
- Rounded rectangles hand-drawn with quadratic curves (no external helper)
- Joystick well from opposite inset shadows; red ball as a ::after with its own glow
- Round amber buttons with a hard 4px bottom shadow and inset highlight that travel
  3px and gain an inset dark shadow on :active
- Keyboard and pointer-capture joystick both feeding one turn function that refuses
  reversing
- Zero-padded four-digit score and high-score display
- Attract-mode repaint interval keeping the food pulsing before play
- 20×20 grid at 15px cells, four-digit LEDs, mono HUD
- Responsive: notes grid to one column under 820px, canvas scales to container width

## Intended interaction model
Insert coin, then drive. Visitors play with the real keyboard (arrows/WASD/Space/R),
by dragging the joystick well, or with the on-screen A/B buttons; the deck buttons
add Start, Pause and Reset. The cabinet is a game first, and the arcade-floor notes
are the flavour text around it.
