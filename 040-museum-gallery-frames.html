Room 4 · 7 October 2026 → 4 January

Nine works,
one room.

Hung at 148 cm from the floor to the centre of each piece, in the order the artist wanted them read, which is not the order they were made.

Curator's note

Read in order, top left to bottom right.

The nine works share one material and one argument. Each artist was given the same square of linen and one colour of the curator's choosing, and told nothing else. The colours were chosen by rolling a die against a nineteenth-century pigment chart, and the resulting order is the order of the hang.

None of the works are for sale. The linen was woven in Ghent in 2025 and stretched by a single workshop in Copenhagen, which is why all nine frames are fractionally different widths.
