i

The grid

Holo foil is a repeating pattern of lines, dots or facets. Change the pattern and you change the diffraction, which is what a printer does when it chooses a screen.

ii

The blend

The foil layer is composited with color-dodge, so it adds light to the art under it instead of covering it. Embossed, the same layer drops to overlay and the foil goes quiet.

iii

The tilt

The card is rotated in 3D by the pointer and the foil offsets against that rotation, which is the part that makes it read as a physical object rather than a picture of one.

iv

The shine

One specular blob follows the pointer under a screen blend. It is why a real sticker reads as laminated even where the foil is subtle.

v

Embossed

Embossed foil is flattened into the stock instead of sitting on it, so the colours lose their punch and the geometry underneath carries the page.

vi

Stock

A one-pixel repeating gradient overlaid at half opacity gives the printed substrate a tooth that no texture file was needed for.
