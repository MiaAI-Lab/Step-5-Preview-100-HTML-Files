# The Pattern Garden

## Short title
The Pattern Garden

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A dark editorial horticulture journal with one large canvas at its centre: seven plants,
each simulated by the actual algorithm that produced its scientific model rather than by a
drawing of it. Lindenmayer's string rewriting grows a fractal tree; Ohno's phyllotaxis
places florets at the golden angle 137.5077 degrees with radius proportional to the square
root of the index, drawing the parastichy spirals that the eye resolves; de Reffye's
stochastic buds decide at every moment whether to wait, flower, sleep or branch, with
age-dependent probabilities; Runions' space colonisation grows tips toward attraction
points and kills them on contact; Weber and Penn's parameterised trunk draws a tree from
taper, levels, apex angle and split ratio; Honda's symmetric binary tree and Palmer's
annual-shingle oak and simple willow close the set. A slow season cycle walks forward and
back through five colour states, and flowers, leaves and fruit follow it. The type is a
warm serif on near-black with a single leaf-green accent; the notes section is a six-cell
grid with roman numerals as the only ornament. Nothing is randomised without a seed, and
the same seed always grows the same plant: it is a garden, not a gif.

## Major visual techniques used
- Canvas 2D with turtle graphics: push/pop stack, angle in degrees, segment length by order
- L-system with a bounded string (240k characters) so the recursion cannot run away
- Phyllotaxis: golden angle, sqrt-index spacing, Fibonacci parastichies drawn as polylines
- Space colonisation: attraction points, nearest visible tip, kill distance, cached tree
- Deterministic LCG seeded per draw so growth is reproducible from a seed number
- Season gradient as five-stop linear interpolation in RGB between sky/ground pairs
- DPR-aware canvas sizing from the parent's layout box, refit on resize
- Roman numeral figures, a leaf-green monochrome, hairline rules, generous leading

## Intended interaction model
Seven plant buttons switch model and reset the cached colonisation tree; six season chips
jump the cycle to spring, summer, autumn or bare and hold it; three sliders drive order
(1-12), branch angle (0-89) and wind (0-100) live; Regrow reseeds the deterministic random
source; Freeze holds time. Pointer movement over the bed leans on the wind and bends the
growing branches. Keys 1-7 choose a plant, + and - change order, f freezes and r reseeds.
A readout reports plant, iteration, segment count, leaf count and wind.
