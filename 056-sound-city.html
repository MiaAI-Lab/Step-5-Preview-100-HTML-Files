House

A marimba figure on a pentatonic scale. Stacks of houses raise the pitch by a step, so a tall street walks up the scale.

2

Spire

Two sine partials an octave apart with a slow decay. Sparse use gives the town its horizon; too many and the sky stops ringing.

3

Works

Filtered noise with a fast pitch drop. It is the percussion of the place and it decides whether the groove lands on the beat.

4

Park

A short triangle chime with a touch of detune, slightly behind the grid. Parks are the rests in the melody.

5

Tempo

The playhead advances one lot every sixteenth at the tempo you set, and the bar counter resets every sixteen steps.

6

Silence

Nothing sounds until you press Play, because browsers keep audio closed until a gesture asks for it.
