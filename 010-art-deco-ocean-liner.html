Accommodation 01

Promenade Class

An outside cabin on B deck with a porthole you can open, a brass bed, and a steward who remembers how you take your tea. Six cabins only; they go first.

RETURN£ 42
Accommodation 02

Sun Deck Suite

Two rooms and a private veranda above the boat deck, with the smoking room at one end and the lido at the other. Includes a permanent place at the captain's table.

RETURN£ 96
Accommodation 03

Tourist Third

Four berths, no porthole, and the company of everyone interesting on board. The dining saloon is the same room, the food is the same kitchen.
