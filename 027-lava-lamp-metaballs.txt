# MAGMA — A Slow Lamp of Rising Wax

## Short title
Lava Lamp Metaballs

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a lava lamp simulation
called MAGMA. No external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no web fonts, no
external images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and
JavaScript inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: a dark room with one lamp. The ground is near-black
violet #0a0510 with a warm orange radial at the lamp's height and a magenta bloom
at the base. The lamp itself is drawn on canvas as a tapered vessel: a closed path
from a narrow shoulder at the top, out through two cubic béziers to a rounded
bottom and back, filled with a vertical four-stop gradient of translucent magenta,
crimson and orange. A hairline rim stroke at 18% pink-white gives the glass its
edge, and a metallic cap and a dark base sit above and below the vessel.

The wax: up to forty-four blobs, each a closed 26-segment noise-ish circle (two
summed sines at 3× and 5× with a per-blob phase) filled with a radial gradient
whose hue is derived from the blob's own temperature — hot wax is bright orange at
hue ~32, cool wax is deep magenta at ~hue 170 of a 300° span — plus a rotated
elliptical specular. Every blob carries a temperature, a velocity, a drift and a
phase. Each frame it is pushed upward by buoyancy (temperature − 0.5) × heat × drag,
its velocity damps at 0.96, its temperature is warmed by the bulb below and cooled
by the top of the vessel according to its depth, and it bounces off the vessel walls
— whose width at a given height is computed by a function that narrows toward the
top, so blobs squeeze into the neck. Blobs that reach the top teleport to the bottom
as hot wax, and blobs that fall past the base wrap to the top as cool.

The merging: for every overlapping pair of blobs, a bright additive neck stroke is
drawn in 'lighter' blend, with alpha and width driven by how deeply the two
overlap — so when two blobs meet they appear to fuse with a bright filament
between them rather than merely touching. All wax is clipped to the vessel path so
nothing escapes the glass. A wide orange radial glow blooms at the base, scaled by
heat.

Layout: a full-viewport canvas with a floating UI overlay whose pointer-events pass
through except on the controls. Top-left is a monospace model label above an italic
serif headline with one line in orange, plus a short paragraph. Top-right is a
monospace meter stack showing the bulb wattage, blob count and heat percentage.
Bottom is a glass tray with three sliders — Heat (buoyancy strength), Viscosity
(damping, 0–92%) and Blobs (6–44) — plus a Night cycle toggle that ramps heat up
and down in a loop, and a Stir button.

Interaction: clicking inside the vessel pushes every nearby blob away from the
cursor, adds vertical velocity, and warms it up — the lamp visibly reacts to being
touched. Stir kicks every blob's velocity and drift and warms them. Heat and
Viscosity change the model live: high heat with low viscosity makes fast fat
blobs, high viscosity makes a slow gloom that barely moves. Night cycle animates
the heat slider continuously so the lamp pulses over about nine seconds. On narrow
screens the paragraph, meters and hint hide.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A lava lamp that runs on a real little model: noisy wax blobs with their own
temperatures, warm at the bulb and cool at the neck, rising by buoyancy and
damped by a viscosity slider, fusing with bright additive filaments wherever they
overlap, all clipped inside a tapered glass vessel that glows orange at its base
and can be stirred by a fingertip.

## Major visual techniques used
- Tapered vessel path from cubic béziers, used both as the fill silhouette and as
  the canvas clip for the wax
- Vessel width as a function of height so blobs squeeze into the narrowing neck
- Noisy blob outlines from two summed sines (3× and 5×) with a per-blob phase
- Temperature-driven colour: hue interpolated from bright orange to deep magenta
- Buoyancy integration with velocity damping and depth-dependent heating/cooling
- Wrap-around teleportation of blobs at the top and bottom of the vessel
- Additive merge filaments ('lighter' blend) with alpha and width from overlap depth
- Wide orange base glow radial whose alpha scales with heat
- Metallic cap and dark base drawn as trapezoid paths
- Click-to-stir with a distance-weighted push, vertical impulse and warming
- Night cycle that animates the heat slider continuously in a ramp
- Viscosity as a global drag factor on rise, drift and wobble
- Live meters for wattage, blob count and heat
- Italic serif headline with one orange line against 8.5px tracked monospace
- Floating glass tray with backdrop-filter blur; extras hidden under 760px

## Intended interaction model
A simulation you can disturb. Visitors watch the wax rise and fall, click inside
the lamp to push it around and warm it, press Stir to churn it, and set how
vigorous it all is with the heat and viscosity sliders or how much wax there is
with the blob count. The Night cycle hands the lamp over to a slow automatic pulse
so it can be left running as ambience.
