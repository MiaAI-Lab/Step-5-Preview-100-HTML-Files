Oliver Witwer 14 · 1968 · serial 41792

Type once.
It is set forever.

A correction fluid for the brave and a fresh sheet for the sensible. The keys are animated; the paper is the only record.

72

characters across

Pica, ten to the inch. The margin was set twice — once at the top of every page, once at the side, and the two were always slightly different.

1

ribbon, two colours

Black on the top half, red on the bottom, switched by a metal foot pedal that nobody in the office could operate without looking down.

4

copies by hand

A sheet of carbon between every page. The fourth copy was barely legible and was, for that reason, the one everybody actually read.
