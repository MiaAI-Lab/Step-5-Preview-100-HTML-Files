# ERG — Nine Hundred Kilometres of Sand

## Short title
Desert Dunes Parallax

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: an animated dune field
called ERG, framed as a meteorological readout from the Grand Erg Oriental. No
external dependencies whatsoever — no CDN, no web fonts, no external images, no
external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and JavaScript inline, works
offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: late sun over sand. Behind the canvas, a CSS sky runs a
six-stop vertical gradient from #2a1508 through #5a2c14 and #9c4a20 to #c9742e,
#d98a40 and finally #8a4a22, with a warm radial bloom at the sun's position and a
cooler red wash low on the left. On top of that, the canvas draws a low sun with a
44%-radius radial glow, then three to eleven dune ridges, back to front. Each
ridge's silhouette is a sum of three sines at different frequencies, phases and
amplitudes — long primary swell, a shorter secondary, and a fine ripple — so the
crest is irregular rather than a smooth arc. Far ridges are paler and cooler, near
ridges darker and warmer, with the vertical gradient computed per ridge from its
depth fraction. Under every crest a soft dark band (a 44px gradient strip repeated
at 7px steps, clipped to the ridge) gives the windward shadow that makes each dune
read as a solid form rather than a silhouette.

The waterless parallax: dragging horizontally accumulates a pan target clamped to
±1, which eases toward the actual at 0.05 per frame. Every ridge then offsets its
own x by pan × W × 0.35 × its own lag, where lag increases with the ridge index — so
dragging walks the dune line and near ridges travel much further than far ones,
giving real depth. When Drift is on, the sine phases advance slowly so the dunes
creep; when off, the field is a static drawing. Wind above 10% streams thirty to
sixty seconds of additive sand streaks across the frame, each a short line whose
length, speed, height and alpha come from a seeded hash, wrapped horizontally.

Layout: a full-viewport canvas over the sky with a floating UI overlay whose
pointer-events pass through except on the controls. Top-left is a monospace
coordinate label above an italic serif headline with one line in sun-gold, plus a
short paragraph. Top-right is a monospace meter stack showing wind in knots, ridge
count and mean grain size. Bottom is a glass tray with three sliders — Wind
(strength), Ridges (3–11) and Relief (amplitude) — plus Drift and Sandstorm
toggles, and a monospace hint.

Interaction: drag to walk the dune line and feel the parallax depth; set wind,
ridge count and relief live; turn Drift off to freeze the field; press Sandstorm to
jump wind to 88% and haze the whole frame with two warm gradient washes. The gauges
recompute every frame, including the mean grain size which rises with wind speed.
On narrow screens the paragraph, meters and hint hide.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A dune field at last light: three to eleven ridges of triple-sine silhouettes,
pale and cool at the back and dark and warm at the front, each with a clipped
windward shadow under its crest, all walking at different parallax rates as you
drag, with a low sun, streaming sand streaks whose length rises with wind, and a
sandstorm that hazes the frame.

## Major visual techniques used
- Triple-sine ridge silhouettes (long primary, shorter secondary, fine ripple) for
  irregular crests
- Per-ridge vertical gradients that pale and cool toward the back, darken and warm
  toward the front
- Clipped windward shadow bands: a 44px gradient strip repeated at 7px steps
- Depth-weighted parallax: per-ridge lag multiplied into a single eased pan value
- Eased pan (0.05 lerp) accumulated from horizontal drag and clamped to ±1
- Live drifting phases when Drift is on; frozen silhouette when off
- CSS sky behind the canvas: six-stop vertical gradient plus two radial washes
- Low sun with a 44%-radius radial glow and a solid disc
- Wind-driven sand streaks from a seeded hash, in additive blend, horizontally
  wrapped, with length and speed scaled by wind
- Sandstorm haze as two warm gradient washes over the whole frame
- Live gauges: wind in knots, ridge count, mean grain size derived from wind
- Non-finite guards on the computed heights
- Italic serif headline with one sun-gold line against 8.5px tracked monospace
- Floating glass tray with backdrop-filter blur; extras hidden under 760px

## Intended interaction model
A place with weather. Visitors set the wind, the number of ridges and the relief,
then drag to walk along the dune line and feel the near dunes move much further
than the far ones. Drift freezes the whole field; Sandstorm jumps to high wind and
hazes the view. The gauges report what the model is currently doing, so it reads as
a live observation rather than a decorative animation.
