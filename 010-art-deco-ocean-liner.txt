# SS AURELIA — Southampton to Rio, 1931

## Short title
Art Deco Ocean Liner

## The exact prompt used to generate this HTML file

Create a completely self-contained single-file HTML page: a booking page for a
fictional 1931 Art Deco ocean liner, the SS AURELIA, on its maiden Southampton to
Rio voyage. No external dependencies of any kind — no CDN, no web fonts, no external
images, no external SVG files, no JavaScript libraries. All CSS and JavaScript
inline, works offline by double-clicking, responsive, valid HTML5.

Artistic direction: 1930s travel poster. Deep ocean teal (#062a3a) to near-black,
cream type, and gold everywhere — gold hairline rules, gold hatched cell overlays,
gold corner brackets. Behind everything a layered sky: a coral glow top-right, a
teal glow bottom-left, a deep diagonal wash, and a huge slowly-rotating fan of thin
gold rays built from a repeating-conic-gradient, radially masked so it only shows
near its origin, blended into the background in screen mode. A low-opacity SVG
turbulence grain sits on top in overlay blend so everything reads as printed ink on
paper.

Layout, top to bottom: a centred hairline "Cunard Aurelia Line" mark flanked by
gold diamonds; a hero with a hand-drawn Art Deco sunburst fan (inline SVG paths, no
external file), a small uppercase eyebrow, and the ship's name in enormous
geometric sans caps (Futura / Century Gothic stack) with a much smaller, very
widely-tracked route line beneath it; a hairline gold rule; a three-cell
accommodation grid built from 1px gaps over a gold background so the gaps read as
rules, each cell carrying a 135° gold hatch overlay, a category number, an
uppercase room name, a paragraph of period prose, and a ruled price row; a
two-column itinerary section with a real table of the passage (dates, ports,
cumulative miles, hover-highlighting to coral) beside a numbered list of what is on
board; a bordered "Book a berth" panel with two gold corner brackets and a gold
gradient button; and a three-part monospace footer with the ship's tonnage and
official number.

Typography: a geometric sans (Futura → Century Gothic → Avenir → system) used at
400 weight with heavy uppercase tracking for all display type — the exact opposite
of the other pages in this set — and a 9–10px wide-tracked monospace for every
label, port, price and catalog number. Line-heights are wide (1.86–2.4) so the
page feels unhurried and expensive.

Colour palette: ocean teal #062a3a, deep navy-ink #04161f, cream #f4ead6, gold
#c8a04a and light gold #e6cd8b, with a single coral #d96a4f as the accent for
hover states and the price.

Interaction: essentially none, and deliberately so. Hovering a passage row turns
the date coral and the text full cream; hovering the gold button lifts it and
intensifies its shadow; clicking it converts the label to a confirmation. The
background rays turn continuously at 24 seconds per revolution. The grid collapses
to one column under 900px.

## One-paragraph description of the intended design
A 1931 ocean-liner poster turned into a booking page: a deep teal night sky with a
slowly turning gold sunburst fan, enormous geometric caps set at wide tracking,
gold hairline rules and hatched panel overlays, a passage itinerary that blooms
coral on hover, and a gold-leaf button that closes the sale.

## Major visual techniques used
- Layered atmospheric background: two offset radial glows plus a deep diagonal
  linear wash
- Slowly rotating Art Deco sunburst from a repeating-conic-gradient masked to a
  radial falloff and blended in screen mode
- Inline SVG decorative sunburst fan (paths + two concentric circles) drawn
  directly in the markup, no external file
- Gold "rules" manufactured from 1px grid gaps over a gold-coloured grid background
- 135° gold hatch overlay on each cell at 9% opacity via a repeating-linear-gradient
- Corner brackets from zero-sided borders on absolutely positioned pseudo-elements
- Extreme uppercase tracking (.2em–.5em) on a geometric sans for that 1930s
  poster spacing
- Printed-ink grain from an inline SVG feTurbulence data-URI in overlay blend
- Table row hover state that recolours only the date cell
- Gold gradient CTA button with lift + shadow intensification on hover
- 3 → 1 column collapse; rotating rays disabled under prefers-reduced-motion

## Intended interaction model
A poster with a single transaction at the end. Visitors read the page as an
advertisement — name, route, prices, itinerary, the promise of the ship — and the
only real action is the gold "Reserve a cabin" button, which confirms in place.
Hovering the itinerary rows is the only other feedback the page gives, and it is
purely decorative.
