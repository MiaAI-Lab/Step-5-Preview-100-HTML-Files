i

Vertical roll

The field sync lets go and the picture slides until it rolls off the top of the tube and comes back at the bottom. Nothing is broken about the picture; only about where it is being put.

ii

Chroma

Colour is carried on a subcarrier and decoded by timing it. Mistime it and the chroma lands on the wrong part of the picture, so every edge grows a coloured ghost that follows the luminance.

iii

Blocks

Compressed video moves in macroblocks, and a lost packet is a whole block the decoder cannot fill in — so it repeats the last one it had, and the picture freezes in a rectangle.

iv

Scanlines

Draw the beam, blank the gap. One dark line for every two rows of pixels is what a real tube shows, and it is why the picture has less contrast than a flat panel would.

v

Tear

A tear is two halves of two different frames in one picture. The slider is how far down the picture the seam travels, and how far the halves are displaced.

vi

Coin

The game is underneath all of it. Insert a coin and you get one hull and twelve waves; the faults are a monitor problem, not a game problem, and the score keeps climbing either way.
