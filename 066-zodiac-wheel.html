i

Thirty degrees

The wheel is 360 divided by twelve, and every sector is exactly 30° wide. That is the whole reason a sign exists as a division: twelve equal slices of the ecliptic, named after the constellations they held two thousand years ago.

ii

Aspects

Conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition. Each is an angle between two signs, and each has a name and a harmony: seventy-two and one hundred twenty ease, ninety and one hundred eighty grate.

iii

Element

Fire, earth, air and water take turns around the wheel, three signs each, so no two neighbours share one. The colours on the wheel are that rule, drawn.

iv

The stars

Each panel draws the real constellation from real coordinates — not a picture. The lines animate once when you open a sign, which is the same reveal a planetarium does.

v

The hand

Gold, pointing at the sign the sun is in at this minute. It moves on its own because the time it reads is the clock on this machine, not a scheduled animation.

vi

Precession

The signs have drifted from their constellations by about one month since the wheel was named, which is why the star maps and the sectors are drawn separately.
